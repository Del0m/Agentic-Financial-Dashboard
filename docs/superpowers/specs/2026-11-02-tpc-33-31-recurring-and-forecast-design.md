## Decisions for the user

Evidence cannot settle these. Each has a recommendation.

1. **Overdue grace.** A monthly bill the history says is due but has not posted: within 5 days it lands tomorrow; later, it is skipped to its next date with an assumption note. Recommend this. If wrong, a bill paid by another means shows a false dip (too short) or a real late bill is missed (too long).
2. **Repeat thresholds.** A repeated dip alerts again at +25% and +$20, or when it comes within 2 days after being 3+ days away (h3 4.5). Recommend; tune on the eval.
3. **Null card due date.** The minimum counts in the surplus but not on the path. Recommend. If wrong, the path is optimistic by one minimum.
4. **Goal day.** A goal pays the day after the first paycheck of its month, or the 1st with no paycheck stream. Recommend. It is a plan assumption; payments are not tracked.
5. **Raw bank descriptor.** Not stored. Recommend not now; every key uses Plaid's `merchant_name`.
6. **Subscriptions as fixed budget lines** (architecture 6.3 and 6.4). Not part of TPC-33. A subscription is a merchant inside `ENTERTAINMENT`, which holds other spending; the false pace flag on a day-5 subscription remains. Recommend a follow-up in M5.

## Goal

**TPC-33.** A detector over our own transactions finds repeating charges and paychecks. A daily run compares with the previous run and alerts on `new_subscription`, `price_increase` and `income_change`. `GET /recurring`, a Recurring card on the dashboard and the chat tool `get_recurring` show them. Accepting `suggest_cancel` lowers next month's line.

**Done when** (ticket): on a synthetic user with planted streams (a monthly subscription, a gym that raises its price, biweekly pay) every stream is detected and the price-increase alert fires on the run after the higher charge. No stream for a merchant with 2 visits. Every test is in `docs/tests/`. Added by this design: stream precision and recall of at least 0.95 on the synthetic profiles, and the noisy-profile gate (departure 2).

**TPC-31.** A forecast of the checking balance with a low path and a high path. A daily run raises `low_balance` when the low path dips under the buffer within 14 days, naming the bill. `monthly_surplus_cents` feeds goals. `GET /forecast`, a ForecastChart card, a buffer setting and the chat tool `get_cash_forecast`.

**Done when** (ticket): on a user whose rent lands 2 days before payday, the `low_balance` alert fires at least 3 days ahead and names rent. Forecast MAE at d+7 and d+14 and band coverage run in the eval. Every test is in `docs/tests/`. Added: with no paycheck stream the forecast says it assumes no income; goals record which surplus they used.

**Out of scope:** Plaid's `/transactions/recurring/get` comparison (a later manual step; needs Sandbox credentials), the `extra_payment` proposal, annual streams, a new page, tracking real goal payments, fixed-line handling of subscriptions in `fit_budget`.

## Design

**Detector** (`budget_engine/recurring.py`, pure). `detect(txns, today, window_days=180)`: keep dates in `[today - 180, today]`; group by (direction, key); form the dominant cluster (amounts within 10% of an amount, 3+ members, more than half the group; otherwise the whole group) and keep any price step (departure 5); require 3+ charges; class by the median gap (7, 14, 15 each +-3, monthly 30 +-5; 14 vs 15 is biweekly when every charge falls on one weekday, else semi-monthly); apply the departure 2 rules; set `typical_cents` (median of a step's charges, else of cluster amounts, or of the last 3 when varying), `last_cents`, `previous_cents` (`None` when the charge just before the latest is within 5% of it, so only the first charge at a price can alert; otherwise the median of the up to 3 cadence charges before the latest, at least 2, only if within 10% of each other, else `None`; for a step over 50% on its second charge, whose first was dropped as a one-off, the median of the cluster), `next_date`, `anchors`, and `status` (`stale` when `today > last + 2 x gap + tolerance`, 65 days for monthly). `project(stream, through)` lists occurrence dates from `next_date` on. `monthly_cents(typical, frequency)` normalises. Callers pass non-removed, non-pending rows from `load_rtxns`, which resolves categories as the rest of the app does.

**Storage.** `recurring_streams` holds one row per `(user_id, direction, merchant_key)`; `alerts` gains `stream_id` and `signal_key` (TPC-33) and `signal_cents`, `signal_days` (TPC-31). The daily run reads the rows, runs `detect`, then in one transaction updates matches, inserts new streams, and marks missing ones `stale` (never deleted, so alert links hold).

**Signals** (`budget_engine/signals.py`, pure). `new_subscription`: inserted this run, outflow, passes `spending()`, not savings, and predates no history (departure 6). `price_increase`: outflow, passes `spending()`, not savings, not a metered utility (detailed category gas and electricity, water, sewage or other utilities; rent, phone and internet stay in), `last_date` advanced and `last >= previous x 1.05` and `last - previous >= 100`. `income_change`: an `INCOME` inflow went `stale`, or its latest amount is 10% below the median of the earlier ones. A stream that returns from `stale` is the same row and silent. Replay guard: `signal_key` = `<kind>|<stream_id>|<token>` (token: `first_seen`, the new `last_date`, or the stale date; a price that falls back and later rises again alerts again); an alert with that key is never written twice.

**Actions.** The model picks from an allowed set; code downgrades to `alert` (the pattern at `budget_graph.py:204-207`). `new_subscription` and `price_increase`: `alert` or `suggest_cancel`, but never for rent, utilities, loans, medical, government or transfer categories. `income_change`: `alert`. `low_balance`: `alert`, `delay_spend` (needs a flexible category at pace 1.0 or more and a budget), `move_from_savings` (needs a savings account that covers it; amount `shortfall + buffer`, rounded up to $5; a proposal only, nothing moves, never when no paycheck stream is assumed).

**Close.** `gather_month` also reads accepted `suggest_cancel` alerts. After the accepted `adjust_next_budget` values, the stream's category line becomes `cancel_limit(limit, monthly)`: the limit minus the stream's monthly amount, rounded up to $5, never above the limit; unchanged when the result would be $0 or less or there is no line. `suggest_cancel` is not in `pending_proposals`, so it does not expire with the month.

**Forecast** (`budget_engine/forecast.py`, pure). Day 0 is today. Events fall in `(today, today + horizon]`: active streams from `project`, uncovered card minimums on their due dates, goals. A card is covered when a card-payment outflow stream's key contains its normalised account name, or the user has one card and one such stream; a wrong pairing errs toward counting twice. Streams that are goal payments (savings transfers or loan payments whose `first_date` is on or after an active goal's `start_date`) are skipped while the goal is active; a stream that merged with an existing one is not caught (known limit). Everyday cost on day `k`: expected `mean x k`; low `mean x k + 1.2816 x sd x sqrt(k/7)`; high `max(mean x k - 1.2816 x sd x sqrt(k/7), 0)`, with `sd` the sample deviation of the last twelve 7-day totals. The dip is the first day within 14 where `low < buffer`; its shortfall is `buffer` minus the lowest low-path balance in those 14 days; its cause is the largest outflow event on or before that day, else "everyday spending". Status is `no_checking` without a depository account whose subtype is `checking` or null, and `no_history` under 28 days of transactions. With no active `INCOME` stream the forecast assumes no income (`assumptions` has `no_income_stream`). The surplus fallback reasons are `no_inflow_stream`, `short_history` (under 65 days or under 3 inflow transactions), `stale_paycheck` and `no_checking`.

**Low-balance alerts.** Key `low_balance|<bill key>|<dip date>`. Before any model call, code compares with the user's latest alert for that bill: suppress unless `shortfall >= 1.25 x previous` and up by $20, or the dip is within 2 days and the earlier alert was for 3+. A dip that slides up to 3 days later is suppressed; a new date beyond that, or earlier, is a new alert. Severity: high when the low path goes under $0, else medium.

**Gateway.** Purposes `recurring` and `cash_flow`, built field by field from allowlists. Outflow rows carry a scrubbed merchant, category, frequency, amounts, next date. Inflow rows carry `INCOME`, frequency and amount only. The dip row carries date, days ahead, shortfall, buffer, the bill (scrubbed merchant or "credit card minimum") and other events as category and amount. No account names or balances. One audit line per signal.

**Chat.** `get_recurring()` and `get_cash_forecast()`, no arguments, so the tool count goes from 5 to 7 (6a.4). The prompt says to state the forecast as a range and to say when no income is assumed.

## Lifecycle

- **Stream.** Absent, then `active` on the run that finds 3 charges (an alert only if departure 6 allows), then `stale` when the pattern stops (an `income_change` alert for pay), then `active` again on the same row once the new charges alone make a stream. One skipped month is not a stop: the stream stays `active` and the next charge continues it. Rows are never deleted.
- **Daily run.** `sync`, `load_state`, then `categorize`, `update_totals`, `detect_recurring`, `forecast_cash`, `detect_overspend` for a user with a budget; `load_state`, `detect_recurring`, `forecast_cash`, `decide_signals` for `no_budget` and `period_ended`. Then `decide_signals`, `log_advice`, `after_advice`. Only an `adjust_next_budget` alert pauses. Before the pause `log_advice` clears signal state so the checkpoint holds no bill name or balance.
- **Alert.** `pending_user`, then `accepted` or `dismissed`. A dip alert is never auto-dismissed, including when the buffer changes.
- **Goal run.** `load_facts` calls `snapshot`; the source is `forecast` unless a fallback reason applies; both figures and the source go to the run summary.
- **Model calls.** A normal day adds 0. A bad day adds 1 per signal. With `n` overspend flags and `s` signals the cap is `2n + s`.

## API

- `GET /recurring` reads stored rows, never recomputes. `{as_of, streams: [{id, direction, label, frequency, amount_kind, typical_cents, last_cents, last_date, next_date, status, change, previous_cents, first_seen}]}`. `label` is the merchant for outflows and "Income" for inflows. Order: changed, active by next date, stale. 200 with `[]` for no history.
- `GET /forecast?days=` (1 to 90) computes live from `sim_today` or today and writes nothing. Returns `today, status, start_cents, buffer_cents, horizon_days, income, days[{date, expected_cents, low_cents, high_cents}], events[{date, label, cents, kind}], dip, monthly_surplus_cents, surplus_source, suggested_extra_cents, notes`. `no_checking` and `no_history` are 200 with an empty `days`.
- `PUT /forecast/buffer {buffer_cents}` stores $0 to $10,000 (422 otherwise, with a bool, a string or a float rejected). Existing alerts are untouched.
- `AlertOut` gains `stream_id`; the goals run summary and `GoalRunOut` gain `surplus_source`.

## UI

Two cards between Goals and Transactions on `app/dashboard/page.tsx`, each with its own fetch, error state and empty text. "Recurring charges": rows of label, frequency, amount ("about" when it varies), next date or "Stopped", badges for changes, a muted "No longer seen" group; no actions (Alerts owns decisions). "Cash forecast": `ForecastChart` (expected line, low and high band, dashed buffer line, markers for bills), headline "between $X and $Y on <date>", a `<details>` table as the text alternative, and a "Keep at least $" input with Save. The Goals page says which source a run used; `DebtPlanCompare` shows "You could add about $X"; `AlertCard` knows the four new kinds and shows the proposed limit for `suggest_cancel`. Copy says "typical range", not a percentage: measured coverage is 41% to 53%.

## Risks and known limits

| Risk | Handling |
|---|---|
| The short-gap, skip, restart, price-step and next-date rules are unmeasured | TPC-33 Task 2 is a gate; the plan stops if the acceptance numbers are missed |
| Forecast error is mostly stream timing and amounts: MAE $81 at d+7 and $69 at d+14 with a stand-in detector, $21 and $38 with perfect streams; coverage 41% to 53% | Report measured numbers; no 80% claim; a planned-dip check rather than a threshold |
| False dip alarms when the true minimum is within $100 to $400 of the buffer (19 to 24 of 140 days at a $200 cushion); none from $1,000 | Repeat rule, "typical range" copy; tune on the eval |
| An irregular earner (no inflow stream) triggered the 14-day alarm in 3 of 17 dates at a $500 cushion (small n) | Assume no income, say so, `alert` only, never `move_from_savings` |
| `days_requested` is 90 in the code (`client.py:50,65`) although the docs say 180; it applies to new Items only | Change both; tell users to relink |
| Real Plaid behaviour is unverified: null `merchant_name` on paychecks, `available_balance` often null, `next_payment_due_date` quality, card purchase double counting | Documented; the Sandbox comparison is a later step |
| A goal's payments merging into an existing savings stream double count until history catches up | Conservative (understates surplus), same direction as TPC-25 |
| 21 goal tests assert measured numbers | Fixture keeps them on the measured path (conflicts table) |
| Jira closes a ticket when a merged PR names it | The index handoff; a PR names only the ticket it completes |
| Subscription false pace flags remain | Decision 6 |
